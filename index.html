Organise pages

Combine PDFs, put the pages in order, turn them the right way up and take out the ones you don't need.

  • Pages keep their full quality and searchable text.
  • Hidden details like the author and edit history aren't carried into the new file.

Click pages to select them. Drag a page to move it, or use Move earlier and Move later.

Edit text

It covers the old text with a box that matches the page, then types your new wording on top in a similar font. It's made for small fixes.

  • Text won't reflow, so keep changes to about the same length. For bigger changes, edit the Word or Excel original and save it as a PDF again.
  • Pages you change are saved as images, so their text can't be selected or searched afterwards. Pages you don't touch stay exactly as they were.
  • Scans have no text to click. Use White out, then Add text.
  • Removing TFNs or bank details? Use the Redact tab instead, as it finds them for you.

    Convert to PDF

    Turn photos and scanned images into one PDF, in the order you want.

    • Works with JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF and BMP images.
    • Word, Excel and Outlook already make perfect PDFs. In Word or Excel, choose File, Save As, then PDF. In Outlook, choose File, Print, then Microsoft Print to PDF.
